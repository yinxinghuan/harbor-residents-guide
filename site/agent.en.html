Energy & training

04

People & experiences

09

Dialogue & relationships

Conversation, shared experiences and character events build relationships and feedback.

Integration goalAuthored relationships can coexist with Agent goals, events and memory.

10

Quests & world changes

Existing tasks give reasons to act: route guidance, bridge repairs that affect passage, opening routes and the market, character events, experiences and commitments.

Integration goalKeep authored goals while Agent events add variation and opportunities.

12

Identity & saves

Personal identity, inventory and relationships have persistence evidence, as do particular catches, bait, plants and observations after re-entry or refresh.

Integration goalSave personal state and shared facts separately, and retain checkable Agent events.
